# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Critique code, not people. Assume good faith until it is clearly absent. Harassment,
discrimination, doxxing and sustained hostility are not welcome here.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Put the project and its users ahead of being right in a thread.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment in public or private channels.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, repeated off-topic promotion, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Bad-faith arguments about whether the rules apply.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any space where
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block contributors who violate this
code. Enforcement decisions are made in the interest of a healthy project, not to win an argument.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact a maintainer privately through their
GitHub profile. Reports will be handled with discretion.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific code of conduct, informed by the spirit of the Contributor
Covenant.
