# Design direction

**Audience.** Hackathon builders, indie hackers, AI engineers and small product teams who need to
find the right open-source building block before everyone else does.

**Feeling.** Precise, editorial, quietly confident. A research desk, not a hype dashboard. Warm
enough to spend an evening in; dark enough at night.

**Direction.** Editorial Swiss, rendered in chocolate. Parchment paper, espresso ink, one signal
vermilion accent. Fraunces display serif, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for data labels. Hairline
rules, asymmetric 12-column grid, oversized numerals, almost no motion. Light and dark themes share
one set of semantic tokens; the dark theme is deep cocoa, not grey glass.

**Signature device.** The *signal scale*: a five-segment vertical bar next to every repository,
paired with a mono rank number and a hairline ledger. It turns ranking into something you can read
at a glance and makes the whole product feel like an instrument.

**References.** The Economist's data pages, Bloomberg Businessweek charts, Are.na, GitHub's own
monospace discipline.

**What we refuse.**
- Dark glassmorphism and neon gradients.
- Three identical feature cards in a row.
- "AI-powered" as a personality.
- Rounded-everything SaaS templates and shadow-heavy cards.
- Fake urgency, fake metrics, bought stars.
